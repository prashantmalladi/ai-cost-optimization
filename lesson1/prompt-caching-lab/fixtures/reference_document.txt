MERIDIAN INTERNAL REFERENCE — DATA RETENTION AND ACCESS CONTROL POLICY (DRAFT v3.2)
Effective for illustration purposes: June 1, 2026 | Classification: Internal Use Only

================================================================================
SECTION 1 — PURPOSE AND SCOPE
================================================================================

1.1 Purpose
This policy defines minimum requirements for retaining business records, controlling
access to sensitive data, and documenting audit trails across Meridian operating units.

1.2 Scope
Applies to all employees, contractors, and automated systems that create, store,
transmit, or destroy records containing customer data, financial data, or compliance
evidence. Excludes publicly marketing materials unless they embed customer examples.

1.3 Definitions
- Record: any persistent artifact (file, database row, log entry, ticket) subject to retention.
- Sensitive Data: PII, payment metadata, authentication secrets, health-related fields.
- Custodian: business owner accountable for a record class.
- System of Record (SoR): authoritative store for a given entity type.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 — RETENTION SCHEDULE
================================================================================

2.1 Default Retention
Unless a more specific schedule applies, operational records must be retained seven (7)
years from the date of last material change. Material change includes status transitions
that affect regulatory reporting.

2.2 Customer Account Records
Account profiles and contractual terms: retain ten (10) years after account closure.
Support transcripts linked to billing disputes: retain eight (8) years.

2.3 Security Logs
Authentication logs, privileged access logs, and change-management logs: retain three (3)
years online in searchable form; archive an additional two (2) years in cold storage.

2.4 Model Training Artifacts
Datasets labeled with customer identifiers must not be used for general model training.
Derived aggregates with k-anonymity threshold k>=50 may be retained indefinitely with
Legal approval documented in ticket LEG-###.

2.5 Deletion Holds
When litigation hold LIT-### is active, automated deletion jobs for affected record
classes must pause within 24 hours of hold issuance. Release requires Legal written notice.

================================================================================
SECTION 3 — ACCESS CONTROL
================================================================================

3.1 Role-Based Access
Access grants follow least privilege. Standing admin access is prohibited except for
break-glass accounts under MFA and session recording.

3.2 Approval Workflow
Production data access beyond read-only analytics requires:
(a) manager approval,
(b) data custodian approval,
(c) Security review for queries exporting >1,000 rows containing PII.

3.3 Third Parties
Vendor access uses time-bound credentials expiring within 72 hours unless renewed.
All vendor sessions must originate from allowlisted IP ranges maintained by Security.

3.4 Segregation of Duties
The same individual must not both deploy production infrastructure changes and approve
their own change record. Emergency changes require retrospective review within 48 hours.

================================================================================
SECTION 4 — ENCRYPTION AND KEY MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

4.1 Data at Rest
Sensitive Data at rest must use AES-256 or equivalent. Keys rotate at least annually
or immediately upon personnel changes affecting key custodians.

4.2 Data in Transit
TLS 1.2 minimum; TLS 1.3 preferred for external endpoints. Internal service mesh may
use mTLS with certificate lifetimes not exceeding 90 days.

4.3 Secrets
Application secrets must not appear in source control. CI pipelines scan for high-entropy
strings and known secret patterns; builds fail on critical findings unless waived by SEC-###.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 — MONITORING AND INCIDENT RESPONSE
================================================================================

5.1 Detective Controls
SIEM alerts fire on: impossible travel logins, privilege escalation, mass export events,
and deletion spikes exceeding baseline by 3 sigma over 7-day rolling window.

5.2 Incident Severity
Sev-1: confirmed unauthorized access to Sensitive Data. Initial executive notification
within 60 minutes. Regulatory notification timelines follow jurisdiction-specific addenda.

5.3 Post-Incident Review
Sev-1 and Sev-2 incidents require root cause analysis within 10 business days including
control gaps, compensating controls, and target completion dates.

================================================================================
SECTION 6 — VENDOR AND SUBPROCESSOR MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

6.1 Due Diligence
New subprocessors handling Sensitive Data require InfoSec questionnaire, SOC 2 Type II
(or equivalent) within 12 months, and contractual breach notification <=72 hours.

6.2 Data Processing Agreements
DPAs must specify processing purpose, retention limits, deletion certification, and audit rights.

6.3 Geographic Restrictions
Primary storage for EU data subjects remains in EU regions unless Standard Contractual
Clauses and Transfer Impact Assessment are on file.

================================================================================
SECTION 7 — EMPLOYEE OBLIGATIONS
================================================================================

7.1 Training
Annual privacy and security training completion is mandatory before production access grants.

7.2 Clean Desk and Device
Full-disk encryption required on laptops. Screen lock <=5 minutes idle. Report lost devices
within 2 hours to Security.

7.3 Acceptable Use
Personal cloud storage of work records is prohibited. Use approved collaboration tools only.

================================================================================
SECTION 8 — AUDIT AND EVIDENCE
================================================================================

8.1 Evidence Packages
For each control in the compliance matrix, custodians maintain: policy excerpt, system
configuration snapshot, sample query output, and named reviewer sign-off quarterly.

8.2 Sampling
Internal audit samples 5% of access grants monthly; exceptions tracked in AUD-### register.

8.3 Non-Compliance Escalation
Repeated failures in the same control domain trigger corrective action plan within 15 days.

================================================================================
SECTION 9 — EXCEPTIONS AND WAIVERS
================================================================================

9.1 Temporary Waivers
Waivers expire in 90 days maximum. Extensions require Risk Committee review.

9.2 Compensating Controls
Each waiver documents compensating controls and residual risk rating (Low/Medium/High).

SECTION 11 — RECORD CLASSIFICATION TAXONOMY
================================================================================

11.1 Classification Levels
All records receive one of four classification labels at creation time:
- PUBLIC: marketing materials approved for external distribution without customer data.
- INTERNAL: business operations data not containing Sensitive Data.
- CONFIDENTIAL: business data that could cause material harm if disclosed; may include
  aggregated analytics without direct identifiers.
- RESTRICTED: contains Sensitive Data as defined in Section 1.3; highest handling bar.

11.2 Record Class Catalog (Authoritative)

Custodians must map every production datastore to exactly one primary record class.

11.3 Reclassification Rules
Reclassification from INTERNAL to RESTRICTED (or higher) requires custodian approval and
Security notification within one business day. Down-classification requires Legal review when
the record ever contained Sensitive Data.

SECTION 13 — JURISDICTION-SPECIFIC ADDENDA
================================================================================

13.1 European Union (GDPR)
- Lawful basis for processing must be documented before collection of EU personal data.
- Data subject access requests (DSARs): acknowledge within 72 hours; fulfill within 30 days
  unless extension documented (max additional 60 days with notice).
- Breach notification to supervisory authority: without undue delay, and where feasible within
  72 hours of awareness, when risk to rights and freedoms is likely.
- Data Protection Impact Assessments required for large-scale processing of special categories
  or systematic monitoring.

13.2 United Kingdom (UK GDPR + DPA 2018)
- Mirrors EU timelines for DSARs unless UK ICO guidance specifies otherwise.
- ICO breach notification: within 72 hours when likely to result in risk to individuals.
- UK data may not be transferred to non-adequate countries without appropriate safeguards.

13.3 United States — California (CCPA/CPRA)
- Consumer opt-out of sale/sharing must be honored within 15 business days of verifiable request.
- Sensitive personal information use limitations require explicit notice at collection.
- Retention disclosures in privacy notice must align with schedules in Section 2.

13.4 United States — HIPAA (where Business Associate)
- Minimum necessary standard applies to PHI access and disclosure.
- Breach notification to covered entity: without unreasonable delay, no later than 60 days
  from discovery of breach.
- Business Associate Agreement must reference permitted uses in Section 6.2 DPA template.

13.5 Cross-Border Transfer Summary Table

| Origin Region | Destination Region | Required Instrument | Approver |
|---------------|-------------------|---------------------|----------|
| EU | US | SCC + TIA on file | Privacy + Legal |
| EU | UK | Adequacy or SCC | Privacy |
| UK | US | UK IDTA or SCC | Privacy + Legal |
| US (CA) | Any | Standard DPA + CPRA addendum | Privacy |
| Any | Restricted countries list | Prohibited unless Risk Committee waiver | Risk Committee |

SECTION 15 — PRIVILEGED ACCESS MANAGEMENT
================================================================================

15.1 Break-Glass Accounts
- Maximum three break-glass accounts per production environment.
- MFA hardware token required; password rotation after each use within 24 hours.
- Session recording mandatory; Security reviews recording within 2 business days.

15.2 Just-In-Time (JIT) Elevation
- Standard production write access uses JIT elevation windows of 4 hours maximum.
- Extensions require re-approval from manager and Security.
- All JIT grants logged to RC-004 with requesting user, approver, scope, and time bounds.

15.3 Quarterly Access Recertification
- Custodians recertify all standing access for their record classes every quarter.
- Orphaned accounts (no login 90 days) suspended automatically pending manager confirmation.

================================================================================
SECTION 16 — BACKUP, RECOVERY, AND MEDIA HANDLING
================================================================================

16.1 Backup Requirements
- RESTRICTED record classes: encrypted backups, geographically separated secondary region.
- Backup access limited to Infrastructure break-glass and backup operators role.
- Restore tests performed semi-annually; results stored in RC-012 audit workpapers.

16.2 Media Sanitization
- Physical media destruction: NIST 800-88 Purge or Destroy methods documented per device.
- Cloud volume deletion: cryptographic erase verified via provider attestation where available.
- Certificates of destruction retained 7 years under RC-012.

16.3 Disaster Recovery
- RTO/RTO targets in Section 14.1 are maximums; critical payment path targets half those values.
- DR failover exercises conducted annually; executive summary due to Risk Committee within 30 days.

================================================================================
SECTION 17 — AI / ML GOVERNANCE
================================================================================

17.1 Training Data Restrictions
- Datasets containing customer identifiers must not enter general-purpose foundation model
  fine-tuning without Legal approval (LEG-###) and documented purpose limitation.
- Synthetic data generation from RESTRICTED sources requires differential privacy review
  when epsilon > 1.0.

17.2 Inference and Logging
- Production inference logs retaining user prompts with PII must follow RC-003 retention unless
  redaction pipeline certified by Privacy.
- Model outputs used for automated decisions affecting customers require human review pathway
  documented in product risk assessment.

17.3 Evaluation and Monitoring
- Bias and safety evaluations required before major model version promotion to production.
- Rollback procedure must complete within 4 hours of confirmed Sev-2 model incident.

SECTION 18 — DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS (DSAR)
================================================================================

18.1 Intake Channels
Verified requests accepted via privacy portal, legal mail, or support escalation queue.
Support agents must not fulfill deletion/export without Privacy Office ticket assignment.

18.2 Fulfillment Timelines
| Request Type | Acknowledgment | Fulfillment Target | Extension Allowed |
|--------------|----------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Access / portability | 72 hours | 30 days | +60 days with notice |
| Deletion | 72 hours | 45 days | +30 days with notice |
| Correction | 72 hours | 30 days | +30 days with notice |
| Restriction | 72 hours | 30 days | Case-by-case |
| Opt-out (CPRA) | 15 business days | 15 business days | No |

18.3 Identity Verification
Minimum two-factor verification for RESTRICTED categories; reduced friction only for
low-risk INTERNAL records with fraud monitoring enabled.

18.4 Deletion Exceptions
Records under litigation hold (Section 2.5), tax hold, or mandatory retention (Section 2)
may be suppressed from active systems but must remain in legal hold archive until release.

APPENDIX C — SAMPLE AUDIT PROCEDURES (EXCERPT)
================================================================================

Procedure AUD-RET-01 — Retention Job Verification
1. Obtain retention configuration export for three randomly selected record classes.
2. Compare configured period to Section 2 schedules and RC catalog mapping (Section 11.2).
3. Select 25 records past nominal retention; verify deletion or hold flag in LegalHold system.
4. Document exceptions in AUD-### register with severity rating.

Procedure AUD-AC-02 — Privileged Access Sampling
1. Pull 5% sample of JIT elevations from RC-004 for the audit month.
2. Verify each has manager approval, Security approval when scope includes PII export.
3. Confirm session recording exists for break-glass activations.
4. Test one orphaned account suspension workflow in IAM staging.

Procedure AUD-VN-03 — Vendor File Completeness
1. Select all vendors onboarded in quarter with RESTRICTED data processing.
2. Verify SOC 2 Type II date within 12 months and InfoSec questionnaire on file.
3. Confirm DPA includes deletion certification language from Section 6.2.
4. Validate IP allowlist entries match Section 3.3 for active vendor integrations.

Procedure AUD-IR-04 — Incident Timeline Reconstruction
1. For each Sev-1 in period, reconstruct timeline from SIEM, tickets, and executive comms.
2. Verify 60-minute executive notification for Sev-1 (CTRL-IR-02).
3. Verify RCA published within 10 business days (CTRL-IR-03).
4. Trace CAP items to owner and target dates in governance tracker.

APPENDIX D — VENDOR RISK TIERS
================================================================================

Tier 1 — Critical Subprocessor
- Processes RESTRICTED data in production path or stores >10,000 customer records.
- Requirements: SOC 2 Type II annually, pen test summary, 24/7 security contact, DR attestation.
- Review cadence: semi-annual meeting + continuous monitoring alerts.

Tier 2 — Material Subprocessor
- Processes CONFIDENTIAL data or intermittent RESTRICTED with tokenization.
- Requirements: SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 within 12 months, InfoSec questionnaire.
- Review cadence: annual questionnaire refresh.

Tier 3 — Low Risk
- INTERNAL data only or no persistent Meridian data storage.
- Requirements: standard contract terms, security addendum acknowledgment.
- Review cadence: triennial or on contract renewal.

Prohibited vendors: any vendor on sanctions list, without breach notification clause,
or refusing audit rights for Tier 1/2 classifications.

================================================================================
APPENDIX E — ENCRYPTION STANDARDS DETAIL
================================================================================

E.1 Approved Algorithms
- Symmetric at rest: AES-256-GCM or AES-256-XTS for volume encryption.
- Asymmetric in transit: TLS 1.2+ with forward secrecy cipher suites; TLS 1.3 preferred.
- Hashing for integrity: SHA-256 minimum; legacy MD5/SHA-1 prohibited for new implementations.
- Key derivation: PBKDF2, scrypt, or Argon2id for password-based derivation.

E.2 Key Management
- Master keys in HSM or cloud KMS with dual control for manual rotation.
- Data encryption keys rotated annually or on custodian personnel change (Section 4.1).
- Key access logged to RC-004; quarterly review of KMS policy bindings.

E.3 Prohibited Practices
- Hard-coded secrets in application repositories (Section 4.3).
- Shared symmetric keys across production and non-production environments.
- Email transmission of unencrypted RESTRICTED attachments.
